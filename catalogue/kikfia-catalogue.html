About KIKFIA

Factory-built houses, designed around you.

KIKFIA designs and supplies custom portable houses for homeowners, investors and businesses.

We pair proven factory-built steel structures with the layout, finishes and features each customer chooses. A backyard studio, a family ADU and a twenty-unit glamping site all start the same way: a conversation about your site, your budget and how the space will be used.

From there we select the right building system, adapt the floor plan and coordinate production, shipping and installation guidance until the house is standing on your land.
