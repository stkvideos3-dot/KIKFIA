## Sources
- Most high-res photos (folders 01–04) were cut out of the "The Box Modular House 2025" catalog pages, with captions and labels left out.
- Folder 05 comes from the Laizhou Dingrong expandable and collapsible container catalogs. They were embedded at low resolution in the PDFs.

## Left out on purpose
- **Xingang 2026 Tiny House catalog:** every page has a "Xingang" watermark across it.
- **Detailed drawing for collapsible container house:** technical drawings only, no photos.
- Any photo showing company signs, banners, brand logos (such as a SANY excavator or a coffee shop sign), info boards in front of the house, or date stamps.

> Tip: these are supplier photos. Before using them in paid ads, ask the factory to confirm in writing that you may use them for marketing.
